What VERDICT Is

VERDICT is a trader evaluation and skill-assessment platform. We provide access to simulated trading environments that replicate the conditions of CFTC-regulated prediction markets, including markets listed on Polymarket. VERDICT does not facilitate, execute, or custody real trades on these venues on your behalf. Our platform is a software service that grants users access to evaluation accounts and, upon passing, access to a funded account with performance-based payouts.

Trading Evaluation Disclosure

The VERDICT evaluation is designed to assess trader skill under conditions that simulate real prediction market behavior. Passing the evaluation requires demonstrated skill in market analysis, risk management, and disciplined execution. Most users do not pass on their first attempt. We recommend the evaluation for users with prior experience trading prediction markets or comparable financial instruments.
