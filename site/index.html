FAQ

Questions you'll ask.

VERDICT is a prediction market trading platform. Prove your edge through our evaluation, then unlock a live trading account. Trade real Polymarket odds, keep 80% of your profits, and withdraw anytime.

Polymarket re-entered the US in December 2025 as a CFTC-Designated Contract Market. VERDICT operates as a skill-based evaluation and trading platform — similar to the framework used by Topstep and Apex for futures. We geoblock restricted states and work with counsel. See our legal disclosures for full details.

VERDICT has a doxxed founding team, full trade transparency, more achievable evaluation targets (10% vs. 20%), and multi-venue support. PropMarket has anonymous founders, no payout proof, and an opaque trading phase.

Polymarket uses UMA's optimistic oracle for resolution. If a market enters a dispute, we hold your relevant P&L for up to 14 days post-final-resolution before crediting. This protects both you and VERDICT from resolution flips.

On-demand after 14 days from first profit, then weekly. Minimum $125. Methods: USDC on Polygon (instant, no fee), USDC on Ethereum ($5 fee), ACH ($0 fee, 2-3 business days), or wire ($25 fee, same-day).

The evaluation uses real-time Polymarket odds with realistic fills and fees. It's a pure skill assessment. Once you pass, you unlock a live trading account where your profits are withdrawable.

Sports, economics, tech, crypto, geopolitics, and culture markets on Polymarket. Election/politics markets are disabled at US launch pending state-by-state clearance. Position sizes are tiered by market depth.

Payouts are 1099-NEC independent contractor income (not capital gains). You'll receive a 1099-NEC for US tax filing. International traders complete a W-8 BEN. We provide a tax FAQ when your account goes live.
